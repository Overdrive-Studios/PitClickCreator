QLab

Everything QLab needs: audio for each cue, vamps and safeties set up, and a script that builds the cues in QLab 5. Whoever runs the show just presses GO.

  1. Unzip into your show folder and keep the files together.
  2. Open QLab 5 and your workspace, and click the cue these should go after.
  3. Open Import into QLab.applescript and press Run. Allow it to control QLab the first time.
  4. Patch output 1 to the in-ear mix.
